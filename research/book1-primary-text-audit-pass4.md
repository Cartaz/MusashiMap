# Book I — Primary-text audit — Pass 4

## Purpose

Final bounded pass focused on finding additional tertiary historical references suitable for the micro-wiki. This pass deliberately does not reopen settled geographic research or expand the wiki for major characters.

## Deliberate exclusions

### Takuan Soho

Excluded from the tertiary micro-wiki. The novel gives him extensive narrative and biographical treatment; this belongs in the main character/story layer rather than a contextual lookup. The primary text itself provides substantial information about him. citeturn3view3

### Ikeda Terumasa / House of Ikeda

Kept as optional historical-context candidates rather than mandatory tertiary entries. Their role becomes important in the Himeji sequence, but adding them now would not materially improve the compact tertiary-reference set.

### Tsujikaze Temma

Not promoted as a historical micro-wiki entry. The primary text presents him as a narrative character; there is no sufficient reason at this stage to treat the reference as a reader-facing historical-context trigger.

### Generic titles, social categories and military terminology

Terms such as daimyo, samurai, ronin, provincial lord, garrison, foot soldiers and similar vocabulary are not individual micro-wiki entities. They belong to general contextual UI if needed, not the named-entity micro-wiki.

## Decision

**Book I tertiary historical-reference audit is now closed.**

Further additions should only be made if a later implementation pass uncovers a concrete reader-facing need or a primary-text contradiction. Do not continue searching for historical names merely for completeness.
