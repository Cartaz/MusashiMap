# Book II — Character Historical-Status Audit

Status: **staging only — production JSON not modified**

## Method

Historical status is assigned only when an external authoritative or specialist source supports the identification. It is not inferred from the existence of a Japanese name, martial-art affiliation, or appearance in Yoshikawa's novel. The novel remains the sole authority for narrative presence; external sources are used only for historical identity/status.

## Important historical chronology finding

Hōzōin Inshun is historically identifiable and is therefore the clearest promotion from the previous `historical/fictionalized — verify` state. The Hōzōin-ryū's own historical page identifies the school as founded by Hōzōin Kakuzenbo Hoin In-ei (-1607), while independent biographical material identifies Inshun as a monk and martial artist active in the early Edo period. These sources support historical status, but do not override the novel's own scene chronology.

## Rule applied to the novel

Historical status does not imply historical accuracy of the character's actions, relationships, dialogue, or exact location. Those remain literary/narrative facts and are mapped from the Book II corpus only.
