### Events

2. A palanquin bearer asks whether he is Miyamoto, then leaves toward Chawan Hill.
3. Musashi visits temples and prays for his sister and for hardship in his own training.
4. He reaches the Kiyomizudera/Hongandō area and is surrounded by porters.
5. Osugi and Uncle Gon arrive by palanquin after pursuing Musashi from Mimasaka.
6. Osugi publicly challenges Takezō/Musashi over the Hon'iden family grievance.
7. Musashi refuses to draw his sword against the elderly pair and says he cannot fight.
8. He walks through the crowd and escapes; the crowd searches unsuccessfully until sunset.
9. A witness later claims he saw Musashi leap over the western wall; this is explicitly presented as an uncertain witness report.

### State

- Musashi: physically at Kiyomizudera/Hongandō, then escapes the temple grounds. Final destination is not established in this chapter.
- Osugi and Uncle Gon: physically at Kiyomizudera/Hongandō while confronting Musashi.

### Important semantic distinction

Musashi's "retreat" must not be encoded as a defeat or as proof of inability. The chapter explicitly presents his refusal to fight and subsequent escape. The reader-facing data should not add a stronger interpretation than the text supports.
