### Events

1. The Yoshioka School is in crisis after Musashi's challenge and defeats.
2. The school receives/retains Musashi as a challenger after he defeats multiple disciples and seriously wounds others.
3. The first Yoshioka champion suffers a severe arm injury; multiple other disciples are defeated.
4. Two wounded Yoshioka fighters die.
5. Musashi refuses to continue fighting until Seijūrō returns.
6. Seijūrō and Tōji return to the school.
7. The school leadership debates whether Seijūrō should fight Musashi directly.
8. The majority decide to ambush Musashi rather than allow an open duel.
9. Seijūrō objects to the proposed ambush on grounds of cowardice/dishonor, but Tōji and the others proceed.
10. Musashi's room appears occupied, but the attackers discover that he has escaped through disturbed floorboards.
11. Yoshioka pursuers chase a fleeing figure through the streets.
12. Near the Kūyadō and Honnōji ruins they capture Matahachi by mistake, believing him to be Musashi.
13. Tōji recognizes Matahachi from the Yomogi Teahouse.
14. The pursuers release Matahachi and continue searching for Musashi.
15. Matahachi establishes that the swordsman is the former Takezō from Miyamoto and decides that he would like to warn him, while feeling ashamed of his own condition.
