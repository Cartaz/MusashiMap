## Method

- Compare the ending state of each chapter with the opening state of the next.
- Distinguish physical presence from mention/reported whereabouts.
- Preserve `unknown` when the text does not establish a destination.
- Reuse existing Book I identities where the text makes the identity continuous.
- Treat extraction duplications as source anomalies, not narrative repetitions.
- Do not promote a geographic reference to a map marker merely because it is named.

## Continuity windows

### Chapter 1 → Chapter 2

- Musashi is reported at the Yoshioka School at the end of Chapter 1 and is physically present there in Chapter 2.
- Seijūrō and Tōji return to the Yoshioka School and become directly involved in the confrontation.
- Matahachi moves from the Yomogi context to the Honnōji/Kūyadō area before being mistaken for Musashi; this is explicit enough to record the state change without inventing an exact route.
- Denshichirō remains away in Ise; this is a reported absence, not a scene presence.

### Chapter 2 → Chapter 3

- Musashi escapes the Yoshioka School with no destination established.
- Chapter 3 places Musashi at Kiyomizudera/Hongandō; the transition is therefore a new established location, not a route that should be retroactively invented.
- Osugi and Uncle Gon arrive in Kyoto after pursuing Musashi from Mimasaka.

### Chapter 3 → Chapter 4

- Musashi leaves the Kiyomizudera/Hongandō confrontation without a confirmed destination.
- Chapter 4 establishes him staying at a cheap inn in the Kyoto area for several weeks.
- Jōtarō becomes attached to Musashi during this chapter and is explicitly established as his follower after obtaining permission from his master.

### Chapter 4 → Chapter 5

- Musashi travels from the Kyoto area toward Nara/Hōzōin.
- Jōtarō is sent back toward Kyoto carrying correspondence and a mission concerning Matahachi.
- Chapter 5 places Jōtarō at Yomogi, where Akemi confirms that Matahachi has left and his destination is unknown.
- Otsū is travelling toward Nara but changes course and travels toward Koyagyū with Shōda Kizaemon.

### Chapter 5 → Chapter 6

- Jōtarō continues toward Nara/Hōzōin.
- Otsū is now associated with the Koyagyū journey, not with Musashi's Nara route.
- Musashi reaches Hōzōin and begins the confrontation with Agon.

### Chapter 6 → Chapter 7

- Musashi remains in the Nara/Hōzōin narrative area after the Agon encounter.
- Jōtarō reaches Musashi with the Yoshioka reply.
- The next chapter moves them toward Hannya Plain.
- Dampachi, Banryū and Yasubei transition from visitors/recruiters to participants in the Hannya Plain confrontation.

### Chapter 7 → Chapter 8

- Approximately ten days after Hannya Plain, Musashi is established in Yagyū territory.
- Musashi and Jōtarō arrive at/near Koyagyū and stay at the local inn.
- Musashi visits Kasagidera and Jōruriji on the way and becomes increasingly interested in meeting Sekishūsai.
- The chapter explicitly establishes Yagyū Valley, Koyagyū Castle/Main House, the Yagyū mountain house and the local inn as distinct locations.

### Chapter 8 → Chapter 9

- Musashi remains in Koyagyū/Yagyū territory.
- Sekishūsai's mountain house becomes the central location for the next narrative phase.
- Otsū is established in the Yagyū household environment, playing the flute for Sekishūsai and living as an accepted guest/outsider.
- Shōda Kizaemon serves as the link between the Yagyū house and the visiting Yoshioka party.

## Chapter 8 source verification notes

Direct repository text confirms:

- Yagyū Valley lies at the foot of Mount Kasagi northeast of Nara.
- Koyagyū is a prosperous rural community organized around the Main House/castle.

- Musashi arrives roughly ten days after Hannya Plain and has visited Kasagidera and Jōruriji.
- Musashi and Jōtarō stay at the local inn.
- Kocha is the young woman working there.
- Musashi overhears the Yoshioka/Denshichirō connection in the bath and understands that the visiting Kyoto group is associated with the Yoshioka house.

## Chapter 9–11 verification status

These chapters have been directly checked against the repository corpus where the connector returned the source text, but the large files are still subject to response truncation. Existing Pass A records remain provisional until the final complete-source verification pass.

### Chapter 9 — The Peony

Confirmed from the repository source:

- Primary setting: Yagyū/Koyagyū, especially Sekishūsai's mountain house and the Wataya inn.
- Sekishūsai's history, retirement and relationship with Lord Kōizumi are extensively narrated.
- Otsū is physically present in the mountain house and is treated as a trusted guest.
- Shōda Kizaemon returns from an errand to the visiting Yoshioka party.
- Denshichirō is explicitly associated with the Yoshioka party at the Wataya.
- Sekishūsai uses Otsū as his messenger to Denshichirō, sending a letter and a peony.
- Otsū travels from Koyagyū Castle to the Wataya on horseback.

The remaining scene sequence requires a complete-source pass before production conversion.

### Chapter 10 — Jōtarō's Revenge

Confirmed from the repository source:

- Jōtarō returns to the inn after carrying out his mission.
- Kocha treats Jōtarō's scratches; the incident involves a Yagyū castle dog.
- Musashi prepares to leave the inn and proceeds toward Koyagyū Castle.
- Musashi is admitted through the main gate on Shōda Kizaemon's invitation.
- The Shin'indō is established as a scholarly/library building within the castle complex.
- Musashi meets Shōda Kizaemon, Kimura, Debuchi and Murata.
- The conversation concerns the peony cut by Sekishūsai and becomes an implicit test of Musashi.
- A disturbance involving the castle dog changes the course of the evening and leads to a search for Jōtarō.

The end of the chapter requires complete-source verification before production conversion.

### Chapter 11 — The Nightingales

Confirmed from the repository source:

- Musashi hides in the moat of Koyagyū Castle after the disturbance.
- He searches the castle grounds through the night for Sekishūsai's mountain house.
- He reaches the secluded mountain-house gate at daybreak.
- The setting causes Musashi to reconsider his intention to force a confrontation.
- Otsū is physically present in the mountain-house grounds and is heard playing the flute.
- Jōtarō is also present and is searching for Musashi with Otsū.
- Otsū and Jōtarō discover Musashi's hand towel, establishing that he has been nearby.

The final encounter sequence must be verified completely before production conversion.
