# Book II — Complete Character Geography & Interaction Scrape

Status: **staging research — no production JSON changes**

Source: canonical Book II chapter files in `data/source/book2/`.

Method: chapter-by-chapter extraction of physical presence, location transitions, co-presence, direct interactions, reported locations, and end-of-chapter continuity. A marker is justified only for physical presence. Reported whereabouts are recorded separately and do not create a marker.

## Confidence scale

- **DIRECT** — explicitly present at the named location in the source text.
- **DIRECT-AREA** — explicitly present, but the exact sub-location is described only as part of a larger area.
- **INFERRED-CONTINUITY** — carried forward from an explicit previous state with no departure shown.
- **REPORTED** — the text gives the person's whereabouts through another character; do not treat as physical presence in the current scene.
- **UNNAMED-GROUP** — physical group is present but individual identities are not supplied.

# b2c1 — The Yoshioka School

## Spatial timeline

1. **Yoshioka School / Shijō Avenue** — Yoshioka Seijūrō, Gion Tōji and the group of eight young Yoshioka samurai leave the school and walk toward the pleasure district. DIRECT.
2. **Kamo River pleasure district** — Seijūrō, Tōji and the students enter the district. DIRECT.
3. **Brothel / drinking house** — Seijūrō, Tōji, Ueda Ryōhei and the other students drink, sing, gamble with cups, and argue. DIRECT.
4. **Same house** — Tōji and Ryōhei physically separate two students after a fight. DIRECT.
5. **Departure toward Yomogi** — Seijūrō secretly leaves the first house; Tōji follows. Ryōhei remains with the other students. DIRECT.
6. **Yomogi Teahouse** — Tōji and Seijūrō arrive. Okō and Akemi are present. DIRECT.
7. **Yomogi interior / veranda** — Tōji interacts with Okō; Seijūrō interacts with Akemi. DIRECT.
8. **Later at Yomogi** — Okō and Tōji negotiate privately; Seijūrō remains with Akemi. DIRECT.
9. **End state** — Seijūrō remains at Yomogi; Tōji sleeps there; Okō and Akemi remain there. Matahachi is present elsewhere inside the establishment. DIRECT.

## Named characters physically present

- Yoshioka Seijūrō — Yoshioka School → Kamo River pleasure district → brothel → Yomogi.
- Gion Tōji — same route as Seijūrō; interacts heavily with Seijūrō, Okō and Akemi.
- Ueda Ryōhei — Yoshioka School/pleasure district house; remains with the group when Seijūrō/Tōji leave.
- Okō — Yomogi.
- Akemi — Yomogi.
- Hon'iden Matahachi — Yomogi, in a dark back room; physically present but separated from the main party.

## Important non-physical references

Yoshioka Kempō, Musashi, Toda Seigen, Ogasawara Genshinsai and Hō Ittōsai are discussed, but are not physical presences in the opening Yoshioka-group scene.

## Interaction graph

- Seijūrō ↔ Tōji: master/retainer, drinking, planning, movement between establishments.
- Tōji ↔ Ryōhei: senior disciples; Tōji/Ryōhei intervene in student fight.
- Seijūrō ↔ unnamed Yoshioka students: drinking and banter.
- Seijūrō ↔ Akemi: flirtation; Akemi resists his physical advances; later drinking together.
